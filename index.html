§ 02 — How we build

Three rules. No exceptions.

01

Private by design

Your data lives on your devices and in your own iCloud. No sign-ups, no ads, and no servers of ours storing what you save.

02

Native, never generic

Built specifically for Apple platforms, so every app feels at home right next to the ones that came with your iPhone and Mac.

03

One job, done well

Each app solves one everyday problem completely, instead of ten problems halfway. Fewer buttons, more calm.
